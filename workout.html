IntelliFit Workout Guide

Training routines for every goal

💪 Muscle Gain

  • Pushups: 3 Sets x 15 Reps
  • Squats: 4 Sets x 12 Reps
  • Deadlifts: Heavy lifting (once a week)
  • Rest: 45-60 seconds between sets

🏃 General Fitness

  • Brisk Walking: 30 mins daily
  • Planks: 3 Sets (60 seconds each)
  • Cycling: 20 mins
  • Yoga: 15 mins for flexibility

🔥 Weight Loss (HIIT)

  • Jumping Jacks: 3 Sets x 50 Reps
  • Burpees: 3 Sets x 10 Reps
  • Running: 2-3 KM daily
  • Mountain Climbers: 3 Sets x 30 Reps
