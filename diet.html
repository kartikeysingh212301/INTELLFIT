IntelliFit Diet Library

Choose a plan that fits your current BMI status

🔵 Underweight (Gain)

Goal: Healthy Weight Gain

  • Drink smoothies & protein shakes.
  • Eat 5-6 smaller meals per day.
  • Include nuts, butter, & whole grains.
  • Add full-fat dairy to your diet.

🟢 Normal (Maintain)

Goal: Stay Fit & Healthy

  • Balance proteins, carbs, & fats.
  • Stay hydrated (3-4L water/day).
  • Focus on fresh fruits & vegetables.
  • Limit processed sugar & junk food.

🟠 Overweight (Loss)

Goal: Fat Loss & Energy

  • High fiber foods (Salads, Beans).
  • Portion control (Eat in smaller plates).
  • Avoid sugary drinks & oily food.
  • Increase protein intake for muscle.
